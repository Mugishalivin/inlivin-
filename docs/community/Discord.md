# Discord Community

## Join Our Community

Connect with fellow creators, share your work, ask questions, and get support from the inlivin community.

### Community Channels

**#announcements**
- Latest platform updates, features, and news
- Product announcements and release notes

**#general**
- General discussion and off-topic conversation
- Meet other creators

**#showcase**
- Share your work and get feedback
- Get inspired by other creators' projects

**#help-support**
- Ask questions about using inlivin
- Get help from community and support team
- Share tips and tricks

**#feature-requests**
- Suggest new features
- Vote on feature ideas
- Discuss product direction

**#marketplace**
- Share your marketplace listings
- Discuss selling and monetization
- Commission announcements

**#collaborations**
- Find collaboration partners
- Discuss project partnerships
- Network with other creators

**#live-events**
- Upcoming live streams and events
- Community meetups
- Workshops and tutorials

## Server Rules

1. **Be Respectful** - Treat all members with courtesy and respect
2. **No Spam** - Avoid excessive self-promotion without community context
3. **No Hate Speech** - Zero tolerance for discrimination
4. **Stay On Topic** - Keep discussions relevant to channels
5. **No NSFW Content** - Keep the community family-friendly
6. **No DMs for Promotion** - Use appropriate channels for announcements

## Getting Support

If you need help:
1. Check pinned messages in relevant channels
2. Use the search function to find answers
3. Ask your question in #help-support
4. DM a moderator if needed

## Community Events

We host regular community events:
- **Monthly Creator Spotlight** - Showcase featured creators
- **Live Q&A Sessions** - Chat with our team
- **Workshops** - Learn new skills from experts
- **Challenges** - Participate in creative competitions
