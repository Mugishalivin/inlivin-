# Features

Discover the powerful features that make inlivin the ultimate platform for artists, creators, and collaborators.

- Showcase your work with customizable artist profiles
- Multiple media format support (audio, video, images, documents)
- Real-time analytics and performance tracking
- Advanced discovery algorithms to reach your audience

### 2. Collaboration Tools
- Real-time collaboration workspace
- Project management and task tracking
- Version control and revision history
- Integrated messaging and comments

### 3. Content Management
- Drag-and-drop content upload
- Batch processing capabilities
- Automated content organization
- Smart tagging and categorization

### 4. Monetization
- Multiple revenue stream options
- Subscription management
- Marketplace integration
- Payment processing

### 5. Community & Networking
- Connect with creators in your niche
- Join communities and groups
- Discover trending content
- Build your fanbase

### 6. Analytics Dashboard
- Real-time performance metrics
- Audience insights
- Engagement tracking
- Revenue analytics

### 7. Live Streaming
- High-quality live broadcast capabilities
- Interactive features (polls, Q&A, chat)
- Recording and replay functionality
- Multi-platform streaming

### 8. Marketplace
- Sell digital products and services
- Commission management
- Inventory tracking
- Automated fulfillment
