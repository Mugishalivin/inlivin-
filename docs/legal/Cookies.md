# Cookies & Tracking Policy

## Cookies & Tracking Policy for Inlivin

**Last Updated: April 2026**

This Cookies & Tracking Policy explains how we use cookies, pixels, and similar tracking technologies on inlivin's website and applications.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that contain information about your browsing activity, preferences, and session data. They help websites remember who you are and how you interact with them.

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### Session Cookies
- Created when you log in
- Deleted when you close your browser
- Enable account authentication
- Maintain your session

### Persistent Cookies
- Stored on your device for a set period
- Remember login information
- Store user preferences
- Track your activity

### Essential Cookies
- Required for basic functionality
- Not optional
- Examples: Authentication, security, fraud prevention

### Performance Cookies
- Measure how you use the Service
- Help us improve performance
- Examples: Page load times, error tracking

### Analytics Cookies
- Track user behavior and patterns
- Help us understand how people use inlivin
- Aggregated data only
- Used with Google Analytics

### Marketing Cookies
- Track marketing campaign effectiveness
- Enable targeted advertising
- Show relevant content
- Placed by third-party partners

### Preference Cookies
- Remember your choices (theme, language, settings)
- Improve personalization
- Enhance user experience

## 3. Tracking Technologies

### Pixels & Web Beacons
- Tiny, invisible images on web pages
- Track page views and user actions
- Used for marketing and analytics

### Local Storage
- JavaScript-based storage on your device
- Persistent across sessions
- Stores user preferences and data

### Tags & SDKs
- Code snippets that track behavior
- Enable third-party integrations
- Comply with your privacy settings

## 6. Do Not Track (DNT)

We honor DNT browser signals:
- If DNT is enabled, we don't set marketing cookies
- Essential cookies are still used
- 3rd parties may not honor DNT

## 8. Cookie Retention

Cookies are retained based on their purpose:
- **Session cookies**: Until browser close
- **Authentication**: 30 days
- **Preferences**: 1 year
- **Analytics**: 2 years
- **Marketing**: 90 days

You can delete cookies anytime.

## 9. Cross-Site Tracking

We use first-party cookies to recognize you across inlivin services. We don't track you across unrelated third-party websites.

## 10. Children's Cookies

For users under 18, we:
- Limit tracking data collection
- Don't use behavioral marketing cookies
- Require parental consent for tracking
