# Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy for Inlivin

**Last Updated: April 2026**

inlivin ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: Name, email, username, password, profile picture, bio
- **Profile Data**: Portfolio items, content preferences, interests
- **Payment Information**: Credit card, billing address, transaction history
- **Communications**: Messages with other creators, support tickets, feedback
- **Content**: Files you upload, descriptions, metadata, comments, posts

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: Device type, OS, browser type, IP address
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, content viewed, actions taken, time spent
- **Cookies & Tracking**: Browser cookies, pixels, tracking codes
- **Location Data**: Approximate location based on IP address (with consent)

### Information from Third Parties

- **Social Media**: When you connect social accounts
- **Payment Processors**: Payment verification and fraud prevention
- **Analytics Providers**: Aggregated usage statistics

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

- **Service Delivery**: Provide, maintain, and improve our platform
- **Account Management**: Create and manage your account
- **Payments**: Process transactions and send receipts
- **Communication**: Send updates, respond to inquiries, support
- **Personalization**: Customize your experience and recommendations
- **Marketing**: Send promotional content (with consent)
- **Analytics**: Understand usage patterns and improve services
- **Safety**: Detect fraud, enforce terms, protect rights
- **Legal**: Comply with laws and regulations

## 3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only:

- **With Service Providers**: For hosting, payments, analytics (under NDAs)
- **Public Profile**: Your public profile data is visible to other users
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, regulation, or court order
- **Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, bankruptcy
- **Your Consent**: When you explicitly authorize sharing
- **Community Features**: Through collaboration and messaging features

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

## 4. Data Protection & Security

We implement:
- SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
- AES-256 encryption for stored data
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Access controls and authentication
- Incident response procedures
- GDPR and CCPA compliance

However, no method is 100% secure. Report security issues to: security@inlivin.com

## 5. Your Rights & Choices

### Access & Portability
- Request a copy of your data
- Export your information in portable formats

### Correction & Deletion
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten)

### Opt-Out & Preferences
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails
- Adjust notification settings
- Disable cookies (may limit functionality)

### Do Not Track
- We honor DNT browser signals where applicable

**To exercise these rights:** Submit a request at privacy@inlivin.com

## 6. Children's Privacy

inlivin is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we'll delete it promptly.

For users 13-18, we provide enhanced protections and parental involvement options.

## 7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to, processed in, and stored in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from your home country.

By using inlivin, you consent to such transfers, subject to appropriate safeguards.

## 8. Retention

We retain your information as long as needed for:
- Service delivery
- Legal obligations
- Dispute resolution

You can request deletion anytime, subject to legal obligations.

## 9. Third-Party Links

inlivin may contain links to third-party websites. We're not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their privacy policies independently.

## 10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes via:
- Email announcement
- Website notice
- In-app notification

Changes effective upon posting.
