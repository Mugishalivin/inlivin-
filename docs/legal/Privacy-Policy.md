# Privacy Policy

**See [Privacy](/docs/legal/Privacy.md) for complete privacy information.**

This is a quick reference guide to our privacy practices.

## Quick Summary

- **What we collect**: Account info, content, usage data, device info
- **How we use it**: Provide service, improve platform, send updates
- **How we protect it**: SSL encryption, secure servers, regular audits
- **Your rights**: Access, delete, opt-out, port your data
- **Contact**: privacy@inlivin.com

## Key Points

### Data Collection
We collect information you provide and information about how you use the service.

### Data Protection
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We comply with GDPR and CCPA.

### Data Sharing
We don't sell your data. We share only with service providers under agreements.

### Your Rights
You can request, correct, delete, or port your data anytime.

### Changes
We'll notify you of significant privacy changes.
