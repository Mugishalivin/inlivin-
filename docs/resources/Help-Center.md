## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General Questions

**What is inlivin?**
inlivin is the platform where artists come alive. Create, collaborate, and connect globally with tools to manage your work, monetize your content, and build your audience.

**Is inlivin free?**
Yes! inlivin has a free Starter plan. We also offer Creator, Pro, and Enterprise plans with additional features.

### Account & Signup

**How do I create an account?**
Visit [inlivin.com](#) and click "Sign Up". You can create an account with email or integrate with social media.

**Can I change my username?**
Yes, you can change your username in Account Settings. Changing it frequently may confuse followers, so we recommend keeping it consistent.

**How do I reset my password?**
On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and follow the instructions sent to your email.

### Content & Uploads

**What file formats do you support?**
- Video: MP4, MOV, AVI, WebM (up to 5GB)
- Audio: MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC (up to 1GB)
- Images: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP (up to 100MB)
- Documents: PDF, DOCX, TXT (up to 50MB)

**What are the upload limits?**
- Starter Plan: 10 uploads/month
- Creator Plan: Unlimited uploads
- Pro & Enterprise: Unlimited uploads

**Can I edit content after uploading?**
Yes! You can edit metadata, descriptions, and thumbnail. Re-uploading requires deleting and re-uploading the file.

### Pricing & Billing

**Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**
Yes, change your plan anytime in Account Settings. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle.

**Do you offer refunds?**
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay.

**Can I get a yearly discount?**
Yes! Choose annual billing at checkout for 20% off.

**When will I be charged?**
You'll be charged on the same date each month or year, depending on your billing cycle.

### Monetization

**How do I start earning?**
Enable monetization in Account Settings and link your payment method. See [Pricing](/docs/product/Pricing.md) for feature availability by plan.

**How much of revenue do I keep?**
inlivin takes a 10% platform fee. You keep 90% of revenue from:
- Subscriptions
- Marketplace sales
- Tipping
- Sponsored content

**When do I get paid?**
Payouts occur monthly on the 15th for earnings from the previous month.

**Are there minimum payout amounts?**
Yes, you need at least $10 in earnings to cash out.

### Collaboration & Teams

**Can multiple people manage one account?**
Yes! With Creator plan and above, you can add team members with different permission levels.

**How many team members can I add?**
- Creator Plan: Up to 3
- Pro Plan: Up to 5
- Enterprise: Unlimited

### Privacy & Security

**Is my content private?**
You control privacy settings for each piece of content. Options include public, private, or followers-only.

**Can I delete my account?**
Yes, you can request account deletion in Account Settings. It takes 30 days to process.

**How is my data protected?**
We use industry-standard encryption, regular security audits, and comply with GDPR and other privacy regulations.
